## 👤 About

I've been coding since 2020, and I build at the intersection of **AI/ML** and **education technology**. My work spans machine learning models, full-stack web applications, and the product thinking needed to turn them into tools people use. I'm the founder of **Beta Startup Builder**, and I'm focused on EdTech products that connect learners with better opportunities and resources.

## 🎯 Focus Areas

- **Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning**: RAG systems, LLM applications, and Python-based ML pipelines
- **EdTech**: Scholarship discovery, course aggregation, and learner-facing tools
- **Full-Stack Development**: Modern web apps with Next.js, React, and TypeScript
- **Startups**: Building products from idea to launch as founder and CEO
